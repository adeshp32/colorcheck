# Privacy Notes

ColorCheck does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics code.

Browser-selected analysis frames and uploaded source files are used only for the requested analysis or export. Original filenames are replaced with generic internal names. Analysis inputs are deleted when analysis finishes; export inputs are deleted when streaming ends, including interrupted streams. Corrected video is streamed from FFmpeg and is never written to server storage. Abandoned upload chunks expire after two hours, while generated reports expire after six hours by default. A hosting provider may still retain ordinary infrastructure logs such as IP addresses, request times, and error metadata under its own policy.

Do not upload footage you do not have permission to process. Do not use the public demo for confidential, regulated, or irreplaceable media.
