# Privacy Notes

ColorCheck does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics code.

Uploaded source files are used only to produce the requested analysis and exports. The app replaces original filenames with generic names and deletes source uploads immediately after processing. Generated reports and corrected exports are temporary and expire automatically; the default retention period is six hours. A hosting provider may still retain ordinary infrastructure logs such as IP addresses, request times, and error metadata under its own policy.

Do not upload footage you do not have permission to process. Do not use the public demo for confidential, regulated, or irreplaceable media.
